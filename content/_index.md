## Biography

I am head of the [Variable Image Acquisition and Processing (VBV)](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de/de/kompetenzen/bildauswertung/variable-bildgewinnung.html) research group at [Fraunhofer IOSB](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de), Karlsruhe, Germany. I received my Dr.-Ing. from [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)](https://www.kit.edu) in 2018 for my work on [light-field-based inspection of transparent objects](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000091872), for which I was awarded the [EMVA Young Professional Award 2019](https://www.emva.org/press/dr-johannes-meyer-receives-emva-young-professional-award-2019/). Prior to joining Fraunhofer IOSB, I worked as Lead Engineer for Computer Vision at ITK Engineering GmbH (Bosch Group). I also teach the graduate course [Computational Imaging](https://computational-imaging.de) at KIT.
